## WHAT ARE BOLLINGER BANDS?

```
Upper Band  = 20 SMA + 2 × Standard Deviation
Middle Band = 20 SMA (the mean)
Lower Band  = 20 SMA - 2 × Standard Deviation

~95% of price action falls within the bands
When price touches outer bands = statistically extreme = mean reversion likely
```

### The Logic

```
Price stretches like a rubber band
↓
Touches lower band = oversold = BUY
↑
Reverts to middle band = take profit

Works because:
- Institutions buy dips in Nifty 50 stocks
- Passive index flows support on dips
- Overreactions get corrected
```

## ENTRY RULES (ALL must be true)

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|------|-----|
| 1 | Price touches/pierces **lower Bollinger Band** | Statistical extreme |
| 2 | **RSI(14) below 35** | Momentum confirms oversold |
| 3 | Price above **200-day SMA** | We buy dips in uptrends, not catch falling knives |
| 4 | **Volume declining** at band touch | Sellers exhausting |
| 5 | **Reversal candle** forms (Hammer, Engulfing) | Buyers stepping in |
| 6 | **20 SMA flat** (not sloping down) | Range-bound, not trending |
| 7 | **VIX < 18** | Low volatility = orderly behavior |
| 8 | **No event** within 3 days | Avoid binary risk |

## POSITION SIZING

```
Risk per trade = 1.5% of capital = ₹22,500 (on ₹15L)
Risk per share = Entry - Stop Loss
Shares = ₹22,500 ÷ Risk per share
```

### Example:
- Entry: ₹1,500
- Stop: ₹1,470 (₹30 risk per share)
- Shares: ₹22,500 ÷ ₹30 = 750 shares
- Investment: 750 × ₹1,500 = ₹11,25,000

## PRE-TRADE CHECKLIST

```
□ Is 20 SMA flat (within ±2% slope)?
□ Is price above 200 SMA?
□ Is price at/below lower Bollinger Band?
□ Is RSI(14) below 35?
□ Is volume declining at band touch?
□ Has reversal candle formed? (Hammer/Engulfing)
□ Is VIX < 18?
□ Any major events within 3 days?
□ Is stock in NIFTY 200 with volume > ₹10 Cr/day?
□ Is risk ≤ 1.5% of capital?
□ Are open positions < 5?

ALL YES → Enter
ANY NO → NO TRADE
```

## ENHANCEMENTS

### Regime Filter
- 200 SMA sloping up: Full conviction
- 200 SMA flat: Normal
- 200 SMA sloping down: Reduce size, tighten stops

### VIX Filter
- VIX < 15: Full activation
- VIX 15-18: Normal, tighter stops
- VIX 18-22: Use 2.5 SD bands
- VIX > 22: Pause strategy

### Double Bollinger Bands
- Add second set at ±1 SD
- Buy zone: Between lower 2 SD and lower 1 SD
- Entry: Price crosses back above lower 1 SD
- Filters out ~30% false signals
