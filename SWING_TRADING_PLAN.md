## POSITION SIZING

### The 1.5% Rule

**Formula:**
```
Position Size = (Capital × Risk%) ÷ (Entry Price − Stop Loss Price)
```

**Example with ₹15L:**
- Capital: ₹12,00,000 (trading portion)
- Risk per trade: 1.5% = ₹18,000
- Stock at ₹500, Stop at ₹460 (₹40 risk/share)
- Position = ₹18,000 ÷ ₹40 = **450 shares**

### 1. MEAN REVERSION STRATEGIES

#### 1.1 Bollinger Band Reversal

**Entry Rules:**
- Stock touches/pierces **lower Bollinger Band (20 SMA, 2 SD)** on daily chart
- RSI(14) below 35 (oversold)
- Price above 200-day SMA (uptrend context)
- Trigger: Enter long on next session open after bullish reversal candle (hammer, bullish engulfing)
- Volume: Delivery percentage > 50% on reversal day

**Exit Rules:**
- T1: Middle Bollinger Band (20 SMA) — close 50%
- T2: Upper Bollinger Band — close remaining 50%
- Stop: 1.5x ATR(14) below reversal candle low, or 3% below entry (tighter)
- Time stop: Exit if no movement to middle band in 8 sessions

**Win Rate:** 55-62% on Nifty 50 constituents | **Avg R:R:** 1.8:1

**India Tips:** Best on large-caps with high institutional ownership (HDFCBANK, INFY, RELIANCE). Avoid when VIX > 22.

#### 1.2 RSI Divergence

**Entry Rules:**
- Bullish divergence: Price makes lower low, RSI(14) makes higher low
- Both pivot points 5+ sessions apart
- Stock above 200-day SMA
- RSI below 40 at second low
- Trigger: Enter on break above resistance trendline connecting two RSI highs
- Volume: Rising on breakout day vs declining during divergence

**Exit Rules:**
- Target: Previous swing high
- Stop: Below most recent swing low
- Trail: Move to breakeven once up 1.5x risk

**Win Rate:** 58-65% (daily chart, Nifty 500) | **Avg R:R:** 1.6:1

#### 1.3 VWAP Reversion

**Entry Rules:**
- Price deviates > 1.5% below VWAP on daily chart
- Stock above 20-day EMA
- Trigger: Enter when price crosses back above VWAP from below
- VWAP sloping upward (intraday chart)

**Exit Rules:**
- Target: 1x deviation distance above VWAP
- Stop: 0.75% below entry
- Exit by 3:15 PM if still open

**Win Rate:** 55-60% | **Best window:** 11:30 AM - 1:00 PM IST

### 2. MOMENTUM BREAKOUT STRATEGIES

#### 2.1 Volume Breakout

**Entry Rules:**
- Stock in consolidation range (max 8-12% range) for 10-20 sessions
- Breakout above resistance on volume >= 1.5x 20-day average
- Delivery percentage > 50% on breakout day
- Stock above 200-day SMA
- Sector index above its 20-day EMA

**Entry Execution:**
- Enter on breakout candle close (not during intraday move)
- If candle closes in upper 1/3 of range = enter next open
- If closes in lower 1/3 = skip (potential false breakout)

**Exit Rules:**
- T1: Range height projected from breakout level — close 50%
- T2: 1.5x range height — close remaining 50%
- Stop: Below midpoint of consolidation range
- Trail: Once up 1x range height, trail to breakout level

**Win Rate:** 52-58% | **Avg R:R:** 2.2:1

**India Tip:** NSE delivery data > 55% on breakout = significantly higher follow-through.

#### 2.2 Volatility Squeeze (Bollinger Band Width)

**Entry Rules:**
- BB Width at lowest point in 20+ sessions
- Previous trend up (price above 20 EMA and 50 EMA)
- RSI between 45-60
- Trigger: Enter long when price breaks above upper BB on volume > 1.5x average
- Confirmation: MACD histogram turns positive

**Exit Rules:**
- Target: 2x BB Width at squeeze completion (from middle band)
- Stop: Below lower BB at entry
- Time stop: Exit after 10 sessions if no target hit

**Win Rate:** 55-60% | **Frequency:** 1-2 setups per month per stock

#### 2.3 52-Week High Breakout

**Entry Rules:**
- New 52-week high with volume > 1.5x 20-day average
- RSI(14) between 60-75
- 60%+ of sector constituents above 50-day EMA
- No major corporate event within 7 trading days

**Entry Execution:**
- Wait for retest of breakout level (previous 52-week high)
- Enter on retest if it holds with bullish reversal candle
- If no retest in 3 sessions = skip

**Exit Rules:**
- T1: 10% above breakout — close 40%
- T2: Trail with 21 EMA
- Stop: 5% below breakout level
- Trail: Once up 8%, move to breakeven; then trail 21 EMA

**Win Rate:** 55-60% (large-caps) | **Avg R:R:** 2.0:1

### 3. SECTOR ROTATION STRATEGY

#### The FADE Model

**F — Flow:** Sectors where RS rank improving over 4-6 weeks
**A — Acceleration:** 6-month RS improving AND 3-month RS rising faster
**D — Divergence:** New leader emerging while former leader losing strength
**E — Entry:** Buy top 2-3 stocks by RS within improving sector

#### Implementation Steps

1. **Weekly RS Scan:** Calculate 20-day RS ratio for 8 NSE sector indices vs Nifty 500
2. **Identify Rotation:** Improving quadrant (RS below 20-week MA but turning up)
3. **Confirm with FII/DII:** 2+ weeks of sustained FII buying in sector
4. **Stock Selection:** Top 2-3 stocks by RS vs sector index, above 50-day EMA
5. **Entry:** When sector RS crosses above 20-week MA AND sector above 20-day EMA
6. **Exit:** RS divergence (lower high in RS while price makes higher high)

**Holding Period:** 8-16 weeks (trending), 4-6 weeks (choppy)

**India Tips:**
- RBI rate cuts = rotate into Banking, NBFCs, Realty
- Union Budget = most violent rotations (position 2-3 weeks before)
- Monsoon outlook = FMCG, two-wheelers, tractors

### 5. OPTIONS-BASED STRATEGIES

#### 5.1 Covered Call

**Setup:** Own stock + sell OTM call (delta 0.20-0.30, 30-45 DTE)

**Example:**
- Hold 550 shares HDFC Bank at ₹1,700
- Sell ₹1,800 Call at ₹25 premium = ₹13,750 income
- If HDFC stays below ₹1,800: keep shares + ₹13,750
- If above: shares called away at ₹1,800, profit = ₹68,750

**Rules:**
- Stock above 50-day EMA
- IV Rank > 30%
- No earnings within 30 days
- Max 5% OTM (conservative), 7-10% OTM (aggressive)

**Win Rate:** 65-75%

#### 5.2 Cash-Secured Put Selling

**Setup:** Sell put + keep cash to buy stock if assigned

**Example:**
- Stock at ₹1,500, want to own at ₹1,400
- Sell ₹1,400 Put at ₹30 premium
- If stays above ₹1,400: keep premium
- If falls: buy at effective cost ₹1,370

**Rules:**
- Only on stocks you want to own
- Delta 0.15-0.25
- Strike at/below strong support
- IV Rank > 40%
- 30-45 DTE

**Win Rate:** 70-80%

#### 5.3 Wheel Strategy

Cash-secured put → assigned → sell covered call → called away → repeat

**Annual Return:** 12-24% in range-bound markets

### 8. MONEY MANAGEMENT STRATEGIES

#### 8.1 Kelly Criterion

```
Kelly % = W - [(1 - W) / R]

W = Win rate
R = Win/Loss ratio
```

**Example:** W = 55%, R = 1.5
Kelly % = 0.55 - (0.45/1.5) = **25%**

**Rule:** Use Half-Kelly (12.5%) or Quarter-Kelly (6.25%) — never Full Kelly.

#### 8.2 Fixed Fractional Sizing

```
Position Size = (Capital x Risk%) / (Entry - Stop Loss)
```

**Auto-reduces** position size during drawdowns — protects against compounding losses.

#### 8.3 Anti-Martingale (Pyramid Scaling)

- **Scale in:** Add to winners only when up 1x initial risk
- **Scale out:** Reduce size by 25% after each losing trade
- **Max pyramid:** Never exceed 3x initial position

#### Universal Risk Rules

|------|--------------|
| Max risk per trade | 1.5% of trading capital |
| Max portfolio heat | 5-8% (cash), 3-5% (F&O) |
| Max single-sector exposure | 25% of capital |
| Breakeven stop | Move to entry once up 1.5x initial risk |
| Daily loss limit | 3% — stop trading for the day |
| Weekly loss limit | 5% — reduce size 50% next week |

#### FII/DII 7-Step Framework

1. Note headline FII/DII net direction and quantum
2. Classify: Dual buy = strongest bullish | Dual sell = most bearish
3. Check F&O: Are FIIs net long/short in index futures?
4. Count streak: 3+ sessions = trend, 5+ sessions = high conviction
5. Compare quantum: Rising = strengthening, declining = weakening
6. Add macro context: DXY, US yields, crude, GIFT Nifty
7. Map to Nifty levels via OI data

#### OI Buildup Classification

| Price | OI Change | Classification | Implication |
|-------|-----------|----------------|-------------|

| Down | Rising | **Short Buildup** | Fresh bearish — downtrend continues |
| Down | Falling | **Long Unwinding** | Longs exiting — downtrend losing steam |
| Up | Falling | **Short Covering** | Shorts covering — rally may be temporary |

### LAYER 1: ICT/SMC CONCEPTS (Where Price Will Go)

These concepts identify where institutions are positioned — your target zones.

#### 1.1 Market Structure (BMS/SMS)

**BMS — Break in Market Structure:**
- Price closes above a swing high (bullish BMS) or below a swing low (bearish BMS)
- **Trade ONLY in the direction of higher-timeframe BMS**
- After BMS, always wait for retracement to 50% or OTE (0.618-0.786 Fibonacci)

**SMS — Shift in Market Structure (Failure Swing):**
- Uptrend: Price fails to break last swing high, then breaks previous swing low = bearish SMS
- Downtrend: Price fails to break last swing low, then breaks previous swing high = bullish SMS
- SMS = trend reversal signal. Use as confirmation, not standalone entry

**Weekly BMS Checklist:**
```
□ Nifty 50 weekly: Is BMS bullish or bearish?
□ Stock weekly: Is it making higher highs/lows (uptrend) or lower highs/lows (downtrend)?
□ 200-day SMA: Price above = long trades only. Below = caution/short bias
□ ADX > 25 = trending. < 20 = ranging. Trade accordingly.
```

#### 1.2 Liquidity Pools (BSL/SSL)

**Why institutions hunt liquidity:**
- Banks need sellers to buy, buyers to sell
- Stop-losses = liquidity = where other traders' orders sit
- Institutions push price INTO these zones to fill their large orders

**Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL):**
- Located above recent swing highs
- Where short-sellers have stop-losses
- Institutions drive price up here to trigger shorts, then reverse

**Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL):**
- Located below recent swing lows
- Where long-buyers have stop-losses
- Institutions drive price down here to trigger longs, then reverse

**Liquidity Hunt Pattern:**
1. Price sweeps above a swing high (triggers BSL)
2. Strong reversal candle (displacement)
3. Price moves in opposite direction
4. This is your ENTRY signal

**Practical Application:**
```
Mark on daily chart:
- All recent swing highs = BSL zones
- All recent swing lows = SSL zones
- Round numbers (₹500, ₹1000, ₹5000) = psychological liquidity
- Previous month's high/low = institutional liquidity
```

#### 1.3 Fair Value Gaps (BISI/SIBI)

**Definition:** Three-candle pattern where candle 1 high/low doesn't overlap candle 3.

**BISI — Buyside Imbalance Sellside Inefficiency (Bullish FVG):**
- Candle 1 high does NOT overlap with candle 3 low
- Price offered buyside, made imbalance, sellside left inefficient
- **This is a BUY ZONE** — price tends to fill these gaps

**SIBI — Sellside Imbalance Buyside Inefficiency (Bearish FVG):**
- Candle 1 low does NOT overlap with candle 3 high
- Price offered sellside, made imbalance, buyside left inefficient
- **This is a SELL ZONE** — price tends to fill these gaps

**Entry Rules for FVG:**
```
Bullish FVG (BISI) Entry:
1. Identify BISI on daily chart (gap between candle 1 high and candle 3 low)
2. Wait for price to retrace INTO the gap
3. Enter at 50% of the FVG (consequent encroachment level)
4. Stop: Below the low of candle 3
5. Target: Next swing high or liquidity pool

Bearish FVG (SIBI) Entry:
1. Identify SIBI on daily chart (gap between candle 1 low and candle 3 high)
2. Wait for price to retrace INTO the gap
3. Enter at 50% of the FVG
4. Stop: Above the high of candle 3
5. Target: Next swing low or liquidity pool
```

**Inversion:** A SIBI that acts as support on the buyside = bullish. A BISI that acts as resistance on the sellside = bearish.

#### 1.4 Displacement

- Sudden, forceful price movement (sequence of long candles with minimal wicks)
- Signals surge in buying/selling pressure, often at liquidity levels
- Displacement almost always creates: (1) Market Structure Shift + (2) Fair Value Gap
- **After displacement, look for the FVG entry**

#### 1.5 Inducement

- Peaks of mini-counter-trends within a larger trend
- Smart Money stop-loss hunting in lower timeframes
- After inducement + liquidity taken, price reverses and continues original trend
- **Don't chase the inducement move — wait for the reversal into OB/FVG**

#### 1.6 OTE — Optimal Trade Entry

- After Inducement → Displacement → BMS → FVG
- Use Fibonacci: **Entry at 61.8%-78.6% retracement** of the expansion range
- This is the highest-probability entry zone in ICT methodology

#### 1.7 Balanced Price Range

- Two FVGs from opposite displacements occurring close together
- Price fluctuates within range, filling both FVGs
- **Trade the breakout once both FVGs are filled**

### LAYER 2: ORDER BLOCKS (Where to Enter)

From Daytradingrauf's ICT Order Block PDF — these are your specific entry zones within the FVG.

#### 2.1 Standard Order Block

**Not every up/down close candle is an order block.** Valid OBs form:
- Inside liquidity pools
- Paired with an FVG
- At higher-timeframe key levels

**Bullish OB:**
- Down-close candle(s) that take sell-side liquidity
- Price trades away from key level + SSL run
- Use down-close candle as valid OB
- **Entry:** Close of the OB candle or mean threshold (midpoint of body)
- **Stop:** Below the low of the OB candle

**Bearish OB:**
- Up-close candle(s) that take buy-side liquidity
- Price trades away from key level + BSL run
- Use up-close candle as valid OB
- **Entry:** Close of the OB candle or mean threshold
- **Stop:** Above the high of the OB candle

**Validation Rule:** "If price is in a hurry to move away from the mean threshold, the OB is valid. If price lingers and doesn't respect the mean, the OB is INVALID — do not trade."

**Ignore wicks of OBs.** The bodies tell you what the market is trying to do.

#### 2.2 Breaker Block

Follows same principles as standard OB but forms when price takes liquidity:
- **Bullish Breaker:** Forms after price takes sell-side liquidity
- **Bearish Breaker:** Forms after price takes buy-side liquidity
- Use at HTF key levels as confirmation of CSD (Change in State of Delivery)

#### 2.3 Rejection Block

- Form at wick highs (bearish) and wick lows (bullish)
- Price respects the rejection block, then distributes
- **Don't trade the rejection block directly** — use it as a reference for distribution legs

#### 2.4 Propulsion Block (Highest Probability)

- Forms after opposing liquidity run + first OB is respected
- The next down-close candle (if bullish) = propulsion block
- Price returns to the block's high and mean threshold, then repels away
- **Entry:** High or mean threshold of the propulsion block
- **Stop:** Low of the propulsion block

#### 2.5 Vacuum Block (Reference Only)

- Forms after gap up/down from news events (FOMC, CPI, earnings)
- Gap = absence of liquidity (vacuum)
- Algorithm trades back into the gap
- **Use as reference, not direct entry** — too unpredictable for swing trading

### LAYER 3: SWING ENTRY TECHNIQUES (How to Execute)

From Frank Ochoa's STP-2, Alan Farley's Master Swing Trader, Dave Landry.

#### 3.1 The 3 Pro-Style Entries

| Entry Type | Risk Level | When to Use | Execution |
|------------|------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Ambush** | High | At clear S/R + confluence zone | Limit order at level where price HASN'T YET traded |
| **Retest** | Medium | After first test of a level (PREFERRED) | Limit order at center of the signal candle/wick |
| **Threshold** | Low | Breakout confirmation | Stop-market order beyond recent high/low |

#### 3.2 Ambush Entry

- Place Limit order at level where price has yet to trade
- High risk factor — requires high confidence
- Best at high confluence zones or established S/R
- **Use sparingly** — only at areas of clear significance

**Example:** Stock at ₹500, clear support at ₹480 with FVG + OB confluence
- Place Ambush Limit at ₹481 (before price gets there)
- If filled: Stop at ₹470 (below OB low)
- Target: ₹510 (0.382 retracement)

#### 3.3 Retest Entry (Preferred for Swing)

- After first test of a level, place Limit order at center of the signal candle/wick
- Medium risk — forces discipline and patience
- **Always try to get better trade location**

**Example:** Stock bounces from ₹480, forms hammer candle with low ₹478, close ₹485
- Retest entry: Place limit at ₹481.50 (center of hammer)
- If next day opens at ₹486 and never fills: move limit to ₹483
- If filled: Stop at ₹478 (below hammer low)

#### 3.4 Threshold Entry

- Place Stop Market order beyond recent high/low
- Lower risk — triggers on breakout
- Use when Ambush/Retest fail or for confirmed breakouts

**Example:** Stock consolidating at ₹490-500 for 10 days
- Place Threshold buy at ₹502 (above ₹500 resistance)
- Stop at ₹490 (below consolidation midpoint)
- Target: ₹520 (range height projected from breakout)

#### 3.5 Intraday Entry Techniques

**Opening Range Breakout (OR Breakout):**
- 30-min opening range is standard
- Place Stop Market order beyond OR high (buy) or low (sell)
- "Safe" confirmed entry
- Avoid if OR is too wide

**Opening Range Reversal:**
- Place Limit order at OR low (buy) or high (sell)
- Tricker but creates more profit potential
- Only use with solid directional conviction

**Value Area Reversal:**
- Allow price to test day's Value Area
- Use Retest entry trigger within the VA
- Must have Higher/Lower Value relationship

### LAYER 3B: SWING SETUPS (What to Look For)

#### 3.6 PEMA Pull-Back (Trend Following)

**Rules:**
- Find stocks where PEMA (Pivot-based EMAs: 13/21/34/55) are "stacked and sloped"
- Wait for price to pull back into trigger zone (between 34 and 55 EMA)
- Enter with Retest or Ambush at trigger zone
- **Only trade during Markup (uptrend) or Markdown (downtrend)**

#### 3.7 VWAP Pull-Back (Value Buy)

**Rules:**
- Stock in strong uptrend, pulls back to VWAP
- Enter when price crosses back above VWAP from below
- VWAP must be sloping upward
- **Best window:** 11:30 AM - 1:00 PM IST

#### 3.8 Inside Value (Breakout from Compression)

**Rules:**
- Inside Day: Current day's range inside prior day's range
- Signals potential breakout (3-5 day momentum move)
- Enter with Threshold entry above Inside Day high
- **Best when occurs during Markup/Markdown phase, not congestion**

#### 3.9 Gap Play (Momentum)

**Rules:**
- Gap occurs outside prior price range/compression
- Must maintain gap integrity (price stays above gap open)
- Enter with OR Breakout on gap day, or Retest next day
- **Full-bodied candle after gap = best case scenario**

#### 3.10 N-Bar Wide Range (Contraction Predictor)

- When current 3-bar range is widest of last 10 periods
- Predicts 40%+ range contraction ahead
- **Success rate: 78%** — use for price forecasting, not direct entry
- Avoid trading during contraction, or trade with closer targets

3. **Regime blindness:** Momentum strategies trained on 2021-2024 bull failed when VIX spiked to 22 in Jan 2026
4. **No kill-switch:** Strategies can have runaway loops. Build kill-switch before first trade
5. **Trading before paper testing:** Minimum 3 months paper trading required
6. **Confusing backtest with reality:** Backtests overestimate by 20-40% (perfect fills assumption)
7. **Position sizing mistakes:** Doubling risk from 2% to 4% caused 20% drawdown (IT Jegan lesson)

## TOOLS & PLATFORMS

### TradingView + Pine Script

**Best for:** Charting + strategy development + rapid prototyping

```pine
//@version=6
strategy("RSI + Supertrend Strategy", overlay=true)
rsiPeriod = input.int(14, title="RSI Period")
rsiBuyLevel = input.int(30, title="RSI Buy Threshold")
rsiSellLevel = input.int(70, title="RSI Sell Threshold")
atrPeriod = input.int(10, title="Supertrend ATR Period")
factor = input.float(3.0, title="Supertrend Multiplier")

rsi = ta.rsi(close, rsiPeriod)
[supertrend, direction] = ta.supertrend(factor, atrPeriod)
isBullish = direction == 1
isBearish = direction == -1

longCondition = (rsi < rsiBuyLevel) and isBullish
if (longCondition) strategy.entry("Buy", strategy.long)
exitCondition = (rsi > rsiSellLevel) or isBearish
if (exitCondition) strategy.close("Buy")
```
